FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSFAQ

Who can apply?

Business Cup 2.0 is open to Bachelor’s and Master’s students.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. You don’t need to come from a specific background or have a specific role. Different skills can contribute to building a strong product.

Do I need a team to apply?

No. You can apply: • with an existing team • solo • looking for a team

How big can my team be?

Teams can have 1–3 people. You can participate solo, as a pair or as a team of three.

Can my teammates be from different universities?

Yes. Cross-university teams are welcome.

What if I don’t have a team?

Apply anyway. If you’re selected and looking for teammates, we’ll help with team matching before the hackathon.

Can I participate solo?

Yes. You can build on your own or request team matching.
